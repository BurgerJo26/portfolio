About

Hello, I'm Joané.

I'm a senior product designer based in Bussum, just outside Amsterdam. I design for software that carries weight: AI compliance, business banking, healthcare. The kind of product where a thoughtful interface can be the difference between a user who trusts the system and one who avoids it.

How I got here

Good design mostly goes unnoticed. That has been my anchor point throughout my career. I design to anticipate what someone is about to do and clear the friction that gets in their way, while keeping the friction that protects them from a mistake they cannot undo.

Six years of that has been spent in regulated, workflow-heavy software: business banking and fintech, AI compliance monitoring, and healthcare payments. I am South African, living in the Netherlands. Growing up around many languages, cultures and economic realities has made my thinking as a designer richer. I ask early who else is going to open this screen, what they already know, and what it costs them to get it wrong.

That way of thinking started with fine arts. You learn to interrogate every choice: why this medium, why this material, what it adds to the message. Every colour, texture and placement changes what the work says and how the viewer reads it.

Designing software is the same question in a different medium. Every position, colour and label changes what an interface means and how it is experienced. How will someone experience the (art)work? What are they trying to achieve? And how do they get there with the least friction possible?
